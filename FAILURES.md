### F16: Kaggle queue stall (2 h+ without a GPU), and queue time logged as GPU time
- **What happened:** after F15 both jobs were accepted and sat in `QUEUED` for over two hours. The local watcher hit its 2 h limit and logged the failure with `gpu_s = 7222`, although the job never ran.
- **Fix:** queue waits are no longer GPU time. A job that times out in the queue logs `gpu_s = 0`, and a finished job logs setup time from its own `report.json` timings instead of wall time. I corrected the one wrong line in `runs.jsonl` (marked with a `note`). The watcher limit went from 2 h to 6 h, and `--resume` re-attaches without losing the queue place.
- **Fallback:** `python -m pipeline.music --colab` writes the same ACE-Step job as a Colab notebook (`outputs/colab/acestep_colab.ipynb`). `--import-zip` brings its results into the same cache, so assembly can't tell which GPU made the score.
- **Lesson:** a free GPU queue isn't a schedule. The deadline plan needs a second provider ready before it's needed.
